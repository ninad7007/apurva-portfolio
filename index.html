About

Hi, I am Apurva. I'm an architect with 2+ years of design experience, now studying for a Master's in BIM and Architecture Management.

I hold a Bachelor's in Architecture with a minor in Sustainable Smart Cities. I focus on concept-driven design grounded in a strong philosophy and in human connection. I take ownership of a design from vision to execution, and I bring strong communication, leadership and teamwork to every project.

As architects, it is our duty to make a difference and build right, because in the end, whatever good things we build end up building us.
